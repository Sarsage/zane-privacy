The Zane — Privacy Policy

App: The Zane (Android, package com.tadej.zane) · Developer: Ambrie Dex · Effective: 24 November 2026 · Updated: 26 November 2026

In short: The Zane does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Your music, tags and playlists stay on your device, and files only travel directly to devices you choose.

What The Zane does not do

  • The Zane does not send any data to the developer or to any server, and it does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • The Zane does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address or contact details.
  • The Zane does not sell or share data with third parties.

Data stored on your device

The Zane keeps the following only on your device: the songs you add (copied to the Music/The Zane folder) and songs you receive (in Download/The Zane), your tags, playlists and mixes, and a security key that identifies your device when sharing. Uninstalling The Zane removes its app data; music files in your Music and Download folders stay until you delete them.

Sharing with nearby devices

The Zane's main feature is sending music directly from one device to another, without the internet. To make that work:

  • Your device's name (for example "Galaxy S21") is shown to nearby devices running The Zane or a compatible app (such as LocalSend) on the same Wi-Fi network, and over Bluetooth, so they can find you. This happens while The Zane's sharing is on.
  • Files are sent only when you choose to send them, and received only after you accept. They go straight to the other device, never through a server, over an encrypted connection whenever the other app supports one (The Zane always does).
  • When you share an album or playlist, its details go with it: song names, artists, album information and, if you leave "Include my tags" on, your tags for those songs.

Once files reach another person's device, they are under that person's control.

Permissions and why The Zane asks for them

  • Music and audio files: to play the music in The Zane's folders.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth, nearby Wi-Fi devices): to find other devices and to create a direct Wi-Fi connection for sharing without a network.
  • Location (Android 12 and older only): those Android versions require it for Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct scanning. The Zane never reads or stores your location.
  • Camera: only to scan QR codes when you receive a song by QR. Images are processed on your device and are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Notifications: to show playback controls, incoming share requests and transfer progress.
  • Network access: for transfers between devices on your local network.

You can deny or revoke any permission in Android's settings; only the related feature stops working.

Children

The Zane is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Security

Transfers between devices running The Zane are encrypted (HTTPS with a key unique to each device). Data on your device is protected by Android's app storage.
